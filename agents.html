Onboarding

From licensed to producing.

The same way we work with families, we bring agents along one phase at a time. Know which phase you're in, and the next step is never a mystery.

Phase One

Get Licensed

Complete pre-licensing hours for your state, pass the Life & Health exam, get fingerprinted, and apply through NIPR.

Phase Two

Get Contracted

Submit contracting through CFC, complete AML and carrier-specific product training, and receive your writing numbers.

Phase Three

Get Trained

Learn the products, the CFC presentation, and how to run an appointment end to end. Ride along before you go alone.

Phase Four

Start Producing

Work your book with support behind you — case design help, carrier selection, and a weekly rhythm that keeps you consistent.

Stay Clean

Compliance, kept current.

None of this is optional, and all of it lapses quietly. Put renewal dates in your calendar the day you complete each one.

AML trainingAnti-money-laundering certification, renewed every two years. Most carriers will not release a writing number without it.
Continuing educationState CE hours before every license renewal, including any ethics requirement your state sets.
E&O coverageErrors and omissions insurance, active and on file. Check the effective dates, not just the certificate.
Product-specific trainingAnnuity suitability and any carrier product training required in the state where the case is written.
Non-resident licensesA separate license for every state you write in. Add it before the appointment, not after.
Replacement rulesIf you are replacing existing coverage, the state replacement forms go in with the application every time.
